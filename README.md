## 🧭 Forward Deployed Engineering

My engineering work often extends beyond implementing predefined tickets.

I work across the full problem-solving lifecycle:

```text
Business / Customer Problem
        ↓
Requirement Discovery
        ↓
Problem Decomposition
        ↓
Technical Architecture
        ↓
Rapid Prototyping
        ↓
Production Implementation
        ↓
Integration & Deployment
        ↓
Testing & Evaluation
        ↓
Feedback & Iteration
        ↓
Measurable Outcome
```

### FDE Skills

* **Customer & Stakeholder Discovery** — turning incomplete or ambiguous requirements into clear technical problems
* **Problem Decomposition** — identifying root causes, constraints, edge cases, and measurable success criteria
* **Solution Architecture** — designing end-to-end systems across frontend, backend, AI, databases, APIs, and cloud infrastructure
* **Rapid Prototyping** — validating ideas quickly before over-engineering the solution
* **Production Engineering** — converting prototypes into reliable, maintainable, scalable applications
* **AI & LLM Integration** — integrating LLMs, RAG pipelines, structured extraction, prompt workflows, and AI-assisted automation
* **System Integration** — connecting existing platforms, APIs, databases, cloud storage, queues, and third-party services
* **Evaluation & Debugging** — investigating AI failures, false positives, hallucinations, retrieval issues, and data-quality problems
* **Customer Feedback Loops** — translating real-world feedback into engineering improvements
* **Cross-Functional Communication** — working between engineering, product, business stakeholders, and customers
* **Technical Leadership** — guiding implementation decisions, architecture, reviews, debugging, and delivery
* **Outcome-Oriented Engineering** — focusing on reliability, performance, accuracy, cost, usability, and business impact
